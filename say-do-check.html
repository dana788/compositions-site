Say/Do Check

Does what you say match what you do?

Eleven short statements about a commitment your organization has made. About four minutes. You will get a plain-language read, three questions to take back to your team, and the 10 Signals Field Guide.

Before you start

Think of one commitment your organization has made: something it has said it will do, or be, for employees, customers, or communities. Answer for that one. Go with your first instinct. "Not sure" is a fair answer and tells you something too.

Nothing is scored or ranked. It is a way to notice, not a verdict.

Part 1 of 3Reach & clarity

Reach & clarity

01The people who need to hear this message are actually receiving it.
02People can explain, in their own words, what we are asking of them.
03The channels we use are ones people can realistically reach in their working day.
04Managers get the information early enough to prepare before their teams ask.

Follow-through

05What leaders say is visible in how decisions get made.
06Budget, staffing, and time have moved to match what we have said is a priority.
07Managers can act on this without waiting for permission or workarounds.
08When something we said turns out to be hard, we tell people what changed.

Conditions & belief

09The people responsible for this have the authority to deliver it.
10The systems and processes behind the message support what it asks of people.
11People across roles and locations describe the same experience, not different ones.

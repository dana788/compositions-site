Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains what information Compositions collects through this website, how it's used, and the choices you have. If anything here is unclear, contact us directly — see below.

Information we collect

We collect information in two ways:

  • Information you provide directly — your name, organization, email address, role, and anything you share through the contact form on this site.
  • Automatically collected information — standard technical data such as browser type, device type, and pages visited, gathered through basic web analytics to understand how the site is used.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry, understand the context of your request, and follow up about a potential engagement. We use aggregated analytics data to understand how visitors use the site and to improve it. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party service providers

This site is hosted and its contact form is processed through third-party service providers (for example, our hosting provider and form-processing tool). These providers may process your information on our behalf, solely to help us operate this site and respond to you. We select providers that maintain reasonable data-protection practices.

Cookies

This site may use minimal cookies necessary for basic functionality and anonymized analytics. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising or cross-site tracking.

Data retention and security

We retain information you submit for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry or maintain a business relationship, and we take reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, to request a correction, or to request deletion. We will respond to reasonable requests promptly.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for business audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 years of age.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of this site after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

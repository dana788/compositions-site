Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information Compositions collects through this website, how it is used, and the choices you have. If anything here is unclear, contact us directly using the details below.

Information we collect

We collect information in two ways:

  • Information you provide directly: your name, organization, work email address, role, and anything you share through the Consult request form, the Say/Do Check, the 10 Signals Field Guide, the portfolio request form, or the Ideas notification form.
  • Automatically collected information: standard technical data such as browser type, device type, and pages visited, gathered through basic web analytics to understand how the site is used.

If you take the Say/Do Check, we receive your first name, work email, and your answers to the eleven statements. The results you see on screen are generated in your browser. They are a plain-language pattern, not a score or a diagnosis.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to respond to your request, to send the resources you asked for, to review requests for access to Dana Middleton's portfolio, and to follow up about a potential engagement. If you choose the optional follow-up notes after the Say/Do Check, we send three short emails and then stop. We use your Say/Do Check answers to understand how the check is being used and to improve it. We use aggregated analytics data to understand how visitors use the site. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party service providers

This site is hosted and its forms are processed through third-party service providers (for example, our hosting provider and form-processing tool). These providers may process your information on our behalf, solely to help us operate this site and respond to you. We select providers that maintain reasonable data-protection practices.

Cookies and browser storage

This site may use minimal cookies necessary for basic functionality and anonymized analytics. It may also use your browser's storage to remember small conveniences, such as that you have already seen the opening animation or already received the Field Guide. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising or cross-site tracking.

Data retention and security

We retain information you submit for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request or maintain a business relationship, and we take reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, to request a correction, to stop follow-up emails, or to request deletion. We will respond to reasonable requests promptly.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for business audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 years of age.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of this site after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Accessibility

Our commitment to an accessible website.

Last updated: October 2026

Compositions is built for leaders navigating consequential decisions, which means the website itself has to be usable by everyone who needs it, regardless of ability or how they reach the web.

What is built into this site

  • Semantic HTML structure throughout, so screen readers and other assistive technology can navigate headings, sections, and landmarks correctly.
  • Full keyboard operability. Navigation, accordions, filters, the Say/Do Check, and forms can all be reached and used without a mouse.
  • Visible focus indicators on every interactive element, rather than suppressing the browser's default focus styling.
  • Descriptive alternative text on meaningful images. Purely decorative imagery is marked so it does not clutter screen-reader output.
  • Motion and animation respect your system's reduced-motion preference. If you have asked your device to minimize motion, this site honors that automatically.
  • Color choices intended to maintain sufficient contrast between text and background throughout the site.

An honest note on where we are

We have built this site with these principles in mind from the start, but we have not yet completed a formal, independent accessibility audit against a specific standard such as WCAG 2.1 AA. We would rather tell you that plainly than claim a level of compliance we have not verified. Closing that gap is an active priority.

Something not working for you?

If you encounter a barrier anywhere on this site, such as content you cannot reach or text you cannot read, we want to know about it and fix it.

Email: hello@compositionsadvisory.com

Please include the page you were on and a brief description of what happened. We will respond as quickly as we can.

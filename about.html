About

Compositions is a Structural Communications Firm.

The firm specializes in consequential organizational commitments — mission and values, culture and workforce, brand and customer promise, strategic change and transformation, and social impact — areas where communication is central, but where the outcome also depends on structures and conditions like leadership, operating practice, stakeholder experience, and evidence.

The Firm

Editorial on the surface. Structural underneath. Human throughout.

Compositions brings those dimensions together so an important promise or priority can be clearly defined, credibly communicated, put into practice, measured, and governed over time.

When you work with Compositions, you're working directly with the person who diagnoses the situation, shapes the strategy, and stands behind the recommendation — not handed off to a rotating team once the engagement begins.

The Name

A portfolio is a composition of assets. An organization is a composition of commitments, decisions, and stakeholder experience. Meaning itself is composed — through words, sequence, and context.

The Meaning of Composition

Composition is the intentional arrangement of distinct elements into a unified whole. In writing, individual sentences may all be accurate yet fail to produce a coherent argument. In design, every image, color, and type choice may be strong yet compete for attention rather than create meaning. In music, each instrument may be played well yet fail to produce harmony.

Applied to organizations, composition is the relationship among what the organization says, what it funds, what it governs, what it rewards, how it makes decisions, how it operates, what stakeholders experience, and what the available evidence can confirm.

The quality of the parts matters. But the arrangement governs what the whole becomes.

The Method

Reveal. Architect. Govern.

Reveal Clarify

What the organization is committing to, what's happening, what stakeholders are experiencing, and what the evidence actually supports.

Architect Design

What the commitment requires across strategy, narrative, communication, activation, alignment, implementation, and measurement.

Govern Sustain

How leadership will own, decide, reinforce, monitor, and adapt the commitment over time.

Dana Middleton

Structural Communications Advisor and Principal, Compositions.

Dana has spent two decades watching the same pattern repeat inside organizations of every size: leadership makes a promise in good faith, funds it seriously, communicates it well — and the organization still can't deliver on it. Culture initiatives that never fully land. Brand promises the front line can't keep. Transformations that get announced twice. She kept noticing that the fix everyone reached for was more communication, and that it was rarely the whole answer.

That pattern is what pulled her across disciplines most organizations keep separate. Strategic communications and brand architecture remain her deepest expertise — sharpened by years in organizational development, culture integration, impact measurement, instructional design, and, perhaps most unexpectedly, social epidemiology: the study of how the conditions surrounding a population shape what happens to it. Applied to an organization instead of a public health crisis, the same discipline asks a useful question — what are the conditions around this commitment, and are they helping it perform, or quietly working against it?

It's an unusual combination. It's also why Compositions exists.

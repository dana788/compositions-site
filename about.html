About

Compositions is a Structural Communications Firm specializing in consequential organizational commitments.

The firm works on mission and values, culture and workforce, brand and customer promise, strategic change and transformation, and social impact — areas where communication is central, but where the outcome also depends on leadership, operating practice, stakeholder experience, and evidence.

The Firm

Editorial on the surface. Structural underneath. Human throughout.

Compositions brings those dimensions together so an important promise or priority can be clearly defined, credibly communicated, put into practice, measured, and governed over time.

The firm is principal-led and network-enabled — Dana directly owns problem framing, diagnosis, commitment strategy, senior communications strategy, and final recommendations, extending delivery through a curated bench of specialists assembled around the commitment rather than a fixed staffing pyramid.

The Name

A portfolio is a composition of assets. An organization is a composition of commitments, decisions, and stakeholder experience. Meaning itself is composed — through words, sequence, and context.

Compositions works at all three levels at once. Even the mark is built the same way: distinct, bounded forms that become one coherent whole.

The quality of the parts matters. But the arrangement governs what the whole becomes.

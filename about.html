About

Compositions is a Strategic Communications Firm.

The firm specializes in consequential organizational commitments: mission and values, culture and workforce, brand and customer promise, strategic change and transformation, and social impact. Communication is central in each. The outcome also depends on the conditions around it, like leadership, day-to-day practice, what people experience, and the evidence.

The Name

A composition is a piece of writing. It is also an arrangement of parts into a whole.

Words. What an organization says, promises, and commits to.

Made True. The arrangement of decisions, resources, authority, and day-to-day practice that decides whether those words become fact.

Compositions treats these as one discipline. A promise is not made true by being well written. It is made true by what surrounds it.

Distinct does not mean disconnected.

Culture, brand, leadership, and measurement each keep their own boundaries. The work is governing how a decision in one affects a commitment carried in another.

Good parts do not guarantee a coherent whole.

Every function can be competent and the organization can still contradict itself when those parts are managed independently.

Boundaries create clarity.

Healthy lines clarify who is responsible. The problem is rarely that boundaries exist. It is that no one is looking across them.

The Composition Principle

The quality of the parts matters. But the arrangement governs what the whole becomes.

Dana Middleton

Strategic Communications Leader

Strategic & Internal Communications | Brand, Culture, Change, & Organizational Effectiveness

Helping Organizations Communicate Through Complexity.

Dana is a strategic and internal communications leader with about 20 years of experience helping organizations communicate through complexity: growth, organizational change, culture, mission-driven, and enterprise initiatives.

Dana helps organizations translate complex priorities, commitments, and change into coordinated communications, programs, systems, and experiences people can understand, use, and act on. Her work is communications-led, with an organizational effectiveness lens that helps her recognize when the message itself needs work and when the communications process is revealing a condition that must also be addressed.

Deepest areas of practice

  • Strategic and integrated communications
  • Internal and employee communications
  • Executive communications
  • Change communications
  • Brand strategy and architecture
  • Culture communications and employer brand
  • Stakeholder communications
  • Communications measurement

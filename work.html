Work

Four connected areas of work — determined by what the commitment requires.

Compositions doesn't sell strategy, communications, alignment, and research as separate disciplines to be purchased individually. They're brought together around one consequential commitment at a time — the scope follows the evidence, not a fixed menu.

01

Strategy & Advisory

Clarifying the commitment, the decision leadership is making, and what success requires.

Clarifying the commitment, the decision leadership is making, and what success requires.

The Work May Include

  • Commitment definition and issue framing
  • Leadership inquiry and stakeholder analysis
  • Mission, values, culture, brand, transformation, and impact strategy
  • Executive alignment and decision support
  • Portfolio analysis and governance recommendations
  • Scope and sequencing

Common Buyer Situations

  • "Leadership hasn't agreed on what this actually means."
  • "We need a point of view before we need a campaign."
  • "This crosses several functions and no one owns the whole."

Communications & Activation

Making the commitment clear, meaningful, usable, and actionable.

Making the commitment clear, meaningful, usable, and actionable.

The Work May Include

  • Narrative development and message architecture
  • Executive and internal communications
  • Employee and stakeholder communication
  • Campaign and activation strategy
  • Leader and manager communication tools
  • Launch and reinforcement

Common Buyer Situations

  • "The commitment hasn't been expressed clearly yet."
  • "Leaders are using inconsistent language."
  • "Stakeholders don't understand what's actually changing."
03

Alignment & Operationalization

Translating the commitment into leadership behavior, decisions, and practice.

Translating the commitment into leadership behavior, decisions, and practice.

The Work May Include

  • Leadership alignment and role clarification
  • Behavior translation and manager enablement
  • Operating-practice and decision-rights alignment
  • Stakeholder-experience design
  • Implementation planning and support
  • Cross-functional coordination and governance

Common Buyer Situations

  • "People know the message but not what to do."
  • "The commitment isn't reflected in our decisions."
  • "Implementation is fragmented across functions."
04

Research, Measurement & Intelligence

Evidence, stakeholder experience, and commitment performance.

Evidence, stakeholder experience, and commitment performance.

The Work May Include

  • Qualitative and quantitative stakeholder research
  • Communication and evidence review
  • Measurement strategy and outcome frameworks
  • Commitment-performance assessment
  • Organizational Commitment Portfolio™ analysis
  • Monitoring, reporting, and interpretation

Common Buyer Situations

  • "We can't tell whether this is actually working."
  • "Our metrics measure activity, not outcomes."
  • "We need to know what to do next, not just what happened."

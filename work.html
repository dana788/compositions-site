How the Work Gets Done

The Problem to Solve / Outcome to Enable

Every engagement starts there, not with a deliverable. Then the work moves through eight stages.

01

Frame

Define what the communication needs to accomplish before deciding what to make.

02

Gather

Collect enough evidence to understand what is actually happening.

03

Arrange

Decide how the communications and what supports them need to work together.

04

Compose

Create the communications, architecture, and tools within scope.

05

Proof

Confirm the work is accurate, supportable, and ready to rely on.

06

Release

Put it where the people who need it have to use it.

07

Read

Find out what happened, for whom, and what it means.

08

Refine

Use what was learned to improve, sustain, or start the next round.

The method stays constant. The depth changes.

When Several Commitments Interact

Organizations rarely carry just one commitment at a time.

Culture, transformation, brand, workforce, values, and social impact may be managed separately. In practice, they often depend on the same leaders, authority, resources, systems, and stakeholder trust.

It helps to see what each is producing, how they reinforce or compete with one another, and what the arrangement produces as a whole. A portfolio-level look at this is planned as a later offering, Change Load Review, and is scoped separately.

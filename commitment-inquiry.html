Commitment Inquiry

You already sense something isn't landing the way it should with your initiatives. The Commitment Inquiry gives you language for what you're seeing, a preliminary read on what's actually happening, and a clear next step — in about 10 minutes.

It starts with one question: what is your organization trying to accomplish, and what does leadership need to understand to actually deliver on it? From there, you'll get a preliminary commitment position profile, real patterns worth examining, and a starting point for what should happen next.

By "commitment," we mean anything your organization has promised, funded, or asked people to count on. If it's something you're trying to make real for people, it qualifies.

What you will receive

  • A preliminary commitment position profile based on your responses
  • What your responses suggest may warrant closer examination
  • A preliminary starting point for further review
  • Instant, downloadable, and emailed copies of your summary

This complimentary inquiry provides preliminary orientation and qualification only. It does not apply the protected Coherence Model, establish findings or causation, recommend an intervention, develop a proposal, or provide complimentary advisory work.

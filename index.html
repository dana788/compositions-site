The Composition Principle

The quality of the parts matters. But the arrangement governs what the whole becomes.

EVERY COMMITMENT HAS CONDITIONS.

IT CARRIES TERMS.

IT DEPENDS ON STRUCTURES.

A commitment isn't sustained by intention or communication alone. It requires something from the organization making it — and it depends on whether the systems surrounding it reinforce it, or quietly work against it.

The Arrangement

The parts may be strong. The arrangement may still work against the result.

Well funded. Clearly communicated. Widely activated. A commitment can be all three and still not hold — because holding depends on whether the parts are working together.

Compositions examines how the parts work together.

What Compositions Does

Four connected areas of work — determined by what the commitment requires.

01 — STRATEGY & ADVISORY

Clarify the commitment

Define what the organization is trying to make true and what the commitment requires.

This work may include: commitment strategy, executive advisory, decision framing, and strategic counsel — across mission and values, culture, brand, transformation, and social impact.

02 — COMMUNICATIONS & ACTIVATION

Make it understood

Create the meaning, communication, and engagement required for people to understand and act.

This work may include: integrated communications, internal and employee communications, executive and leadership communications, and change communications.

03 — ALIGNMENT & OPERATIONALIZATION

Translate it into practice

Translate the commitment into leadership, decisions, systems, behaviors, and operating practice.

This work may include: commitment operationalization, mission and values operationalization, leadership and cross-functional alignment, and change operationalization.

04 — RESEARCH, MEASUREMENT & INTELLIGENCE

Know if it's working

Determine what stakeholders experience, what the evidence supports, and whether the commitment is performing.

This work may include: stakeholder research, commitment coherence assessment, performance measurement, and commitment portfolio analysis.

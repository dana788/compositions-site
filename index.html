The Composition Principle

The quality of the parts matters. But the arrangement governs what the whole becomes.

A commitment can be well funded, thoughtfully communicated, and widely activated, and still underperform. What matters is whether decisions, resources, authority, systems, and communication all reinforce what people were asked to trust.

Communicatedclear, well-received
Fundedreal budget, real support
Operationalizedtranslated into practice
Experiencedwhat people encounter
Measuredwhether it's actually working

Recognizable Situations

Three situations, told plainly.

Global, multi-brand organization

One priority, three regions, more than twenty brands.

What was happeningA single priority had to reach more than 3,000 employees across three regions, each brand with its own voice.

What we didDesigned an internal campaign that held one core story and let each brand and region carry it their way, with manager materials built in.

Evidence status: Designed. Outcome not retained.

Frontline manufacturing

The message lived on a laptop. The workforce didn't.

What was happeningMuch of the workforce worked on the production floor, not at a desk, so email-first communication left them out.

What we didBuilt the plan around where people are: printed flyers in break rooms, electronic boards, supervisor talking points with set delivery dates, and reminders.

Evidence status: Implemented.

Culture & employee experience

The average can hide who the promise reached.

What was happeningAn organization wanted to know whether its culture was being experienced the same way by everyone it described.

What we didCleaned and broke the data out by group, shaped the findings into a plain narrative, and built a "you said, we heard, we did" loop for employees.

Evidence status: Implemented. Performance record.

When It's More Than One

When one commitment isn't the whole question.

Organizations often ask the same leaders, managers, systems, and resources to carry several commitments at once. It helps to look at what each is producing, where they reinforce or compete with one another, and who governs the whole.
